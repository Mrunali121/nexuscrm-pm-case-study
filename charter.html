Project Charter

Scope, objectives, team, milestones and success criteria.

1. Project Overview

NexusCRM is an internal B2B SaaS customer management platform replacing Meridian Software's legacy contact database and spreadsheet-based sales tracking. Built and delivered as two releases over 16 weeks by a 9-person cross-functional team.

2. Objectives

  • Replace the legacy contact database and sales spreadsheets with a single system of record for customer and deal data.
  • Cut monthly reporting effort from ~6 person-hours to under 1 (via live dashboards).
  • Reduce duplicate contact records from ~18% to under 3% via a guided data migration.
  • Ship a foundation that supports a future email and Zapier integration layer.
  • Meet GDPR requirements as data controller for EU customer records (data minimization, access logging, right-to-erasure support).

3. Scope

In scope: - Release 1: Auth & permissions, contact/account management, deal pipeline, core admin - Release 2: Reporting dashboards, email integration, Zapier integration, legacy data migration tool

Out of scope (explicitly deferred): - Customer-facing self-service portal - Mobile native app (responsive web only for MVP) - Multi-currency support (single-currency for initial rollout) - Any future external productization (flagged in Business Case as a separate initiative)

Governance: Steering Committee (Sponsor + Engineering Director + Head of Customer Success) meets at each release boundary for go/no-go decisions. PM reports weekly status to Sponsor; escalations follow the path defined in the RACI matrix.

5. Milestones

Milestone Target
Charter approved Week 0
Release 1 (Core CRM) live internally Week 8
Release 2 (Reporting + Integrations) live internally Week 16
Legacy system decommissioned Week 18 (2-week parallel-run buffer)

6. Budget

~420 person-days across the 16-week build, within existing team capacity — no external contractor spend, aside from an external GDPR/security audit ahead of Release 2 go-live (see Dependency Management).

7. Success Criteria

Metric Target
Both releases shipped on schedule Week 8 / Week 16
Duplicate contact rate post-migration < 3%
Monthly reporting effort < 1 person-hour
Critical bugs open at go-live 0
GDPR/security audit Passed prior to Release 2 go-live

8. Constraints & Assumptions

  • Fixed team, no additional hires during the project.
  • EU data residency is mandatory — hosting stays on Meridian's existing EU infrastructure.
  • Legacy system must remain operational in parallel until data migration is verified.
