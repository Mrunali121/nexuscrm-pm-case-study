Roadmap

Two releases, sprint by sprint.

16-week build across two releases, followed by a 2-week parallel-run buffer before the legacy system is decommissioned.

Release 1 — Core CRM (Weeks 3–8)

Goal: Replace the legacy contact database and spreadsheets with a working system of record for contacts, accounts, and deal pipeline.

Sprint Weeks Focus
Sprint 1 3–4 Auth, permissions, environment & CI/CD setup
Sprint 2 5–6 Contact & account management
Sprint 3 6–7 Deal pipeline (kanban board, stage logic)
Sprint 4 7–8 Admin tools, bulk import, audit logging, hardening

Release 1 exit criteria: Internal UAT passed, 0 critical bugs, Sponsor go/no-go approved.

Release 2 — Reporting & Integrations (Weeks 9–16)

Goal: Give leadership live reporting and connect NexusCRM to email and downstream automation via Zapier, plus migrate all legacy data.

Sprint Weeks Focus
Sprint 5 9–10 Reporting engine & live dashboard
Sprint 6 11–12 Report exports, rep/team performance views
Sprint 7 13–14 Email integration, GDPR erasure workflow
Sprint 8 15–16 Zapier integration, data migration tool, backups

Release 2 exit criteria: External security/GDPR audit passed, UAT passed (Sales + Customer Success), 0 critical bugs, Sponsor go/no-go approved.

Post-Release (Weeks 17–18)

  • Parallel-run: legacy system stays live as fallback while NexusCRM is monitored under real usage.
  • Legacy system decommissioned once migration is verified stable.

Explicitly deferred (not on this roadmap)

  • Customer-facing self-service portal
  • Native mobile app
  • Multi-currency support
  • Any external productization decision (owned by a separate future initiative)

See Release Plan for detailed scope and success criteria per release, and Roadmap Chart for the visual timeline.
