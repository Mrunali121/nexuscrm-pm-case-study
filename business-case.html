Business Case

Why build it: the problem, the options considered, and the recommendation.

Company: Meridian Software (B2B SaaS vendor) Prepared by: Project Manager Audience: Executive Steering Committee

1. Problem Statement

Meridian's existing customer management tooling is a patchwork of a legacy in-house contact database and three disconnected spreadsheets used by Sales and Customer Success. This causes: - Duplicate and stale customer records (est. 18% of contact records are duplicates) - No single view of deal pipeline across the 14-person sales team - Manual, error-prone monthly reporting to leadership (avg. 6 person-hours/month) - Inability to integrate with email or third-party tools, blocking a planned Customer Success automation initiative

2. Market Opportunity

Customer interviews (9 existing accounts) and a competitive review of 4 comparable SMB CRM products confirmed demand for a lightweight, integration-friendly CRM as both an internal tool and — pending internal rollout success — a potential future customer-facing product line.

3. Options Considered

Option Pros Cons
A — Buy an existing CRM (e.g. HubSpot, Pipedrive) Fast to deploy, proven Recurring per-seat cost at scale, limited fit with internal data model, GDPR data residency review required for any EU customer data touched by a third party
B — Build NexusCRM in-house Full control of data residency (important given GDPR and EU customer base), extensible foundation for future product line, one-time investment Requires internal engineering capacity, longer time to first value
C — Do nothing No cost Status quo problems compound; blocks the CS automation initiative already committed to leadership

Recommendation: Option B. The one-time build cost is recovered within ~14 months via reduced reporting overhead and reclaimed sales admin time, and it removes the GDPR data-residency question entirely by keeping customer data in Meridian's existing EU-hosted infrastructure.

4. High-Level Cost-Benefit

Estimate
One-time build cost (team time, 16 weeks) ~420 person-days
Ongoing hosting/maintenance Comparable to current legacy DB hosting
Annual savings (reporting time, reduced duplicate cleanup, no per-seat licensing) ~70 person-days/year
Payback period ~14 months

5. Strategic Fit

Directly enables the CS automation initiative already on the FY roadmap, and establishes a data foundation that could later be productized — flagged to the Steering Committee as a possible Phase 3 (post-MVP) direction, explicitly out of scope for this project.

6. Recommendation

Approve NexusCRM as a 16-week, two-release internal build, governed by the attached Project Charter.
